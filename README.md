# Hi, I'm Yajnesh Rao 👋

### AI Safety & Security Researcher

I research how **AI systems fail, how they can be manipulated, and how we can evaluate them more effectively** before they are trusted with increasingly consequential tasks.

My current work focuses primarily on the **security and robustness of Large Language Models (LLMs)**, particularly adversarial attacks, jailbreaks, prompt injection, safety evaluation, and emerging security challenges in agentic AI systems.

## 🧪 Current Research

### Adversarial Robustness & Security Evaluation of Large Language Models

I'm currently investigating how consistently LLM safety mechanisms perform when exposed to adversarial prompting and variations of potentially unsafe requests.

The research explores:

`Adversarial Prompting` · `Jailbreaks` · `Prompt Obfuscation` · `Safety Consistency` · `Model Comparison` · `Failure Severity`

The goal is to better understand **where current safety mechanisms fail and how those failures can be evaluated systematically.**

> 📂 Research repository, dataset and results coming soon.

## 📄 Publication

### Explicit Content Generation in Text-to-Image Models: Training Mechanisms, Moderation Failures, and Safety Limits

Research examining why text-to-image generative AI systems can produce explicit or unsafe content despite existing moderation mechanisms, and the limitations of current approaches to AI safety.

My broader research direction now focuses on **LLM security, adversarial evaluation and agentic AI safety.**
